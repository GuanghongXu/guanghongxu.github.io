Welcome! 

I’m an Economist at [Amazon](https://www.amazon.science/author/guanghong-xu), applying causal inference techniques—both experimental and observational—to quantify advertising effectiveness on Amazon and optimize media investment strategies.

Previously, I worked in East Africa for four years, designing and directing randomized field experiments (RCTs) across Kenya (Economist at [CGIAR International Livestock Research Institute (ILRI)](https://www.ilri.org/) for 36 months), Rwanda (Research Associate at [Innovations for Poverty Action (IPA)](https://poverty-action.org/) for 10 months), and Tanzania (1 month).

My academic research lies at the intersection of Development Economics, Applied Econometrics, and Bayesian Statistics, with a particular focus on the economic development of lower-income countries. I study issues related to technology adoption, quality upgrading, value chains, and financial inclusion.
